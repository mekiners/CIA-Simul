AAD = arrival aerodrome. The aircraft has no route yet: select it and assign a STAR or special route in Aircraft control. Blank time = it appears just outside the 60 NM radar. Times are 24 h Zulu.
DAD = departure aerodrome. ATD = actual time of departure; blank = takes off now. The aircraft starts at zero speed on the runway in use and flies the runway heading (straight out) until you assign a SID or route. Click the aircraft in the Departures list to change its ATD (delay).
